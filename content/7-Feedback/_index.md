### Overall evaluation

**1. Working environment**  
The working environment was friendly, open, and supportive. Members of the FCAJ program were willing to help whenever I encountered difficulties, including outside normal working hours. The workspace and collaboration atmosphere made it easier to stay focused. However, it would be even better if there were more team bonding or informal sharing sessions so that members could understand each other more closely.

**2. Support from mentors and team admins**  
The mentors provided detailed guidance, explained concepts clearly when I did not understand them, and encouraged me to explore solutions on my own. The admin team also supported procedures, documentation, and coordination so that the working process remained smooth. I especially appreciated that mentors did not simply give ready-made answers, but instead created opportunities for me to think and solve problems independently.

**3. Relevance between assigned work and academic background**  
The tasks assigned to me matched the knowledge I had learned at university while also extending into areas I had not yet explored in depth. Because of that, I was able to reinforce my foundation and at the same time gain practical experience from a real project.

**4. Learning and skill-development opportunities**  
During the internship, I learned many new skills such as using project management tools, collaborating across roles, and communicating professionally in a technical team. Mentors also shared real project experience, which helped me better understand possible future directions in cloud and AI-related work.

**5. Culture and team spirit**  
The culture of the program was positive. Team members respected each other, worked seriously, and still maintained a comfortable atmosphere. When the team had urgent tasks, everyone supported one another regardless of role. That made me feel included as a real member of the team rather than only an intern.

**6. Policies and support for interns**  
The program created favorable conditions for interns in terms of guidance, learning resources, and flexibility when needed. The chance to participate in internal knowledge-sharing sessions was also a meaningful benefit.
